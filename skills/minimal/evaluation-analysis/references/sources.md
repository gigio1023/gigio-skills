# Sources and Scope

Reviewed November 4, 2026 while authoring the grain checks, the clustered and paired analysis, the multiplicity rules, and `scripts/eval_compare.py`.

- [Evan Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals", arXiv 2411.00640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640): read in full. Adopted the standard error over items, the clustered standard error (equation 4) with the cluster count reported beside the item count, the variance of a K-sample item mean, the inconsistency of a standard error pooled over all K x N answers, paired item-level differences and their variance reduction, the advice against lowering temperature to cut noise, and the power formula as the hand-off to evaluation-design. Not adopted: next-token probability scoring, which applies only to single-token answers.
- [Sida I. Wang, "Measuring all the noises of LLM Evals", arXiv 2512.21326 v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21326): read the definitions and findings. Adopted data noise and prediction noise under the law of total variance, paired comparison, all-pairs comparison as one declared option for several models, the finding that paired prediction noise usually exceeds paired data noise, and the rough SE(A - B) guide as a sanity check. Not adopted: leaderboard-wide all-pairs estimation as a default.
- [Sam Bowyer, Laurence Aitchison, Desi R. Ivanova, "Don't Use the CLT in LLM Evals With Fewer Than a Few Hundred Datapoints", arXiv 2503.01747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01747): read sections 2 and 3. Adopted the under-coverage of CLT intervals below a few hundred items, Wilson or Beta posterior intervals for independent binary items, and the result that clustered CLT and cluster bootstrap intervals under-cover with small clustered data. Not adopted: importance-sampling Bayesian models as a default, which the script does not implement.
- [UK AISI Inspect](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_ai) at `3321736` (2026-11-04), `src/inspect_ai/scorer/_metrics/std.py`, `src/inspect_ai/scorer/_metric.py`, and `src/inspect_ai/_eval/task/epochs.py`: inspected `stderr`, `ci`, `ci_wilson`, `value_to_float`, and the epochs reducer. Adopted the C/(C-1) clustered standard error, t with C-1 degrees of freedom and cluster bootstrap for clustered intervals, and averaging epochs per sample before metrics. Recorded as pitfalls: NOANSWER maps to 0 and PARTIAL to 0.5 by default, and the `ci` docstring names interval overlap as a use, which a paired interval replaces.
- [EleutherAI lm-evaluation-harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness) at `d6de8164` (2026-09-14), `lm_eval/api/metrics.py` and `docs/task_guide.md`: `mean_stderr` treats documents as independent, and repeated generations are often reduced with the `take_first` filter. Recorded as pitfalls only.
- [K-Dense scientific-agent-skills, `statistical-analysis`](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/tree/main/skills/statistical-analysis) at `a25cf9e` (2026-11-02, MIT): inspected the skill and its assumption-check script, and ran its quick routes in the fit trial. Adopted from its integrity rules: estimand, sampling unit, and multiplicity family declared before looking; no test switching on a diagnostic p-value; effect size with uncertainty first; missingness understood before dropping; a seeded, rerunnable script. Not adopted: APA reporting, the pingouin dependency, and quick routes for binary outcomes that treat rows or (item, sample) pairs as independent, which under-covered in the trial.
- [Anthropic knowledge-work-plugins, `data/skills/validate-data`](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) at `d900840` (2026-11-03, Apache-2.0): its pitfall catalog (join explosion, denominator shifting, average of averages) informed the join and averaging checks. Its confidence scale and report template were not adopted; verdicts belong to evaluation-audit.
- [PostHog skills, `skills/omnibus/diagnosing-experiment-health`](https://github.com/PostHog/skills) at `d755e47` (2026-11-03), its interpretation reference on peeking and multiple comparisons: informed the multiplicity and peeking cautions. Product-analytics procedures were not adopted.
- [OpenAI plugins, `data-analytics`](https://github.com/openai/plugins) at `b767f8f` (2026-11-03): proprietary; read for ideas only, which overlapped the checks above. No text or structure was taken.

The fit trial (synthetic data, 3 models x 300 prompts x 3 samples, 60 behaviors in 6 categories, 400 regenerated item sets) produced the coverage table in `SKILL.md` and the 1/sqrt(nR) comparison; `scripts/eval_compare.py` reproduces its clustered and paired point estimates, standard errors, intervals, noise split, and per-category Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg values on the same table. The Student t distribution in `scripts/eval_stats.py` was checked against scipy 1.18.1 (quantiles within 1e-10 relative, two-sided p-values within 2e-11) and the Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg adjustments against statsmodels 0.15.0. The script's structure and all text here are independently authored. The sources do not establish that these instructions improve agent behavior.
